Ski Guide: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 · Contact: jani.jesenovecj@gmail.com

Ski Guide is an iPhone app for ski mountain maps, weather, snow and ski stats. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and who can see it.

We do not sell data, show ads, use analytics or tracking SDKs, or track you across other apps or websites.

Where your data is stored

All account data is stored with Apple iCloud (CloudKit) under your own Apple ID. We do not run our own servers. Weather, snow and map data are downloaded from public sources (NOAA, USGS, OpenStreetMap). Those requests include the map area you are viewing, but nothing that identifies you.

Your choices

  • Location: you can turn My location off at any time, and stop sharing with a friend by removing them. The app needs only "While Using" permission.
  • Block and report: you can decline any friend request, or block the person so they can't send you requests again. Any post can be reported, and you can hide all posts from a person, from the post's ••• menu. Reports go to the contact address above.
  • Posts: choose Friends or Public for each post, and delete any of your posts from its ••• menu.
  • Delete your account: go to Friends → Add friend → Delete my account. This removes your username, shared location, profile, friend connections and pending requests from iCloud, and signs you out. Your posts, their ratings and your thumbs up are deleted from iCloud too. Stats kept on your phone stay there until you delete the app. To also disconnect your Apple ID, go to Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → Ski Guide → Stop Using.
  • Sign out: Friends → Add friend → Sign out. Friends stop seeing you; your username is kept for when you sign in again.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes, and change the date at the top.
