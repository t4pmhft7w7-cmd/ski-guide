Ski Guide

3D maps of ski mountains with live wind, snowpack and runs, your ski stats and badges, Ski sessions you can replay, and friends on the mountain.

Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback: email jani.jesenovecj@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

Common questions

  • My location isn't showing. Turn on My location in the app, and allow location for Ski Guide in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.
  • Friends or posts don't load. Social features use your iCloud account: check you're signed in to iCloud in Settings and have a connection.
  • Restoring purchases. In the app, open a paywall (Ski Together or the Ski Trip Planner) and tap Restore purchases.
  • Reporting a person or a post. Use Report on a friend request, a friend's stats, or a post's ••• menu, or email us.
  • Deleting your account. In the app: Friends › Add friend › Delete my account. See the privacy policy for what's removed.

Data sources

Weather and snow: NOAA / National Weather Service and NOHRSC. Terrain and imagery: USGS. Ski runs, lifts and lodges: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Resort names identify places only; Ski Guide isn't affiliated with any resort.
